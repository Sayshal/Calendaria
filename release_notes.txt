## Bug Fixes

- Fixed calendar settings failing to register when stored calendar overrides contained corrupted legacy deletion entries; those entries are now repaired on load
- Fixed FXMaster token trails forcing the particle background on when Particle Background is off; per-scene FXMaster overrides are now read from the scene being synced
- Fixed months with a fixed starting weekday not restarting the weekday count each month; only that month's intercalary and non-weekday festival days are counted, the HUD, date formatting, and all calendar views share one weekday calculation, and week-cycle calendars restart the cycle at fixed-start months
