## New Features

- CalendariaAPI.advanceTime gains smooth, duration and fps options for gradual time advancement
- Core's game.time.calendar.format() now routes through Calendaria's token engine
- Moon sliders in the calendar editor show their value while dragging
- New FXMaster Above Darkness toggle on the Weather settings tab, with a per-scene override
- New Remove Calendar Notes button on the Notes tab, also offered when Calendaria is disabled, deleting festival notes or every calendar note
- Note cleanup clears festival seed records and each note's wrapper journal
- Seven weather presets gained their own ambient loop, and gravewind no longer shares the wind ambience
- The [[countdown]] enricher accepts time=HH:MM to count down to a clock time
- timeToComponents now also returns day, leapYear and season

## Bug Fixes

- Bastion turn advancement matches the dnd5e 6.0.x contract, and monthless calendars no longer produce NaN dates across a year boundary
- Imported seasons carry a canonical seasonalType, so solstice detection, note recurrence and icon fallbacks resolve on non-English season names
- Intraday temperatures follow diurnal bands, with the daily headline read from the afternoon
- Monthless calendars honor the configured week start
- Season icons on calendars imported from Simple Calendar and Seasons & Stars now render, with a migration for existing imports
- Timekeeper jump buttons and the unit picker follow the Timekeeper's own increment instead of the shared clock increment
- timeToComponents clamps month and dayOfMonth to the last valid month instead of returning an out-of-range index

## Changes

- Checkboxes and radios follow the atlas theme instead of Foundry's core duotone layer
- The shared wind ambience was re-encoded from 4.2 MB to 1.9 MB
