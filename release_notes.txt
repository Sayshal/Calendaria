## Bug Fixes

- Calendar grids no longer show intercalary month days as leading days from the previous month, and Chronicle weekday names follow the same rule
- The Chronicle now resets to its default date range when the category filter, subject filter or Show Empty Days changes, instead of keeping an ever-growing range
- Overlapping weather changes, standalone sound calls and audio unlock waits are now queued so they no longer leave extra sound loops playing
- Weekday date format tokens (E, e, d, dddd and similar) are now blank on intercalary month days and festival days that do not count for the week
